\begin{abstract}
Heterogeneous multi-UAV task allocation is often solved with classical bipartite matching (e.g., the Hungarian method) when the full task set is known and objectives reduce to distance-versus-capability costs.
We show that this myopic optimum weakens under \emph{Windowed Pop-up Strike} (WPS) dynamics: hard time windows, delayed and local sensing, burst arrivals, and attrition.
We study hybrid allocators that keep classical assignment as the engine while a learned module reshapes inputs: task priorities and soft reserve (MLP-RAH / Att-RAH), timed agent commits (Att-Commit), or agent--task pair scores for coalition escort (Att-Coalition), trained on WPS-aligned scores \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) and \(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\).
On WPS\_hard (100 episodes), Att-RAH significantly improves \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) over visibility-masked Local-Hungarian (paired bootstrap 95\% CI excludes zero); a Global-Hungarian oracle remains an upper bound.
A multi-front suite (WPS\_attn) widens the oracle gap but does \emph{not} separate Att-RAH from MLP or urgency ablations.
Follow-on suites that change the decision interface---timed commits with rematch cost (WPS\_commit) and coalition escort with pair scoring (WPS\_escort)---likewise do not yield attention-specific gains over matched MLP or market baselines under the tested budgets.
We release metrics, figures, and training scripts aligned with the reported tables.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Multi-UAV mission planners routinely cast allocation as bipartite matching between agents and tasks~\cite{kuhn1955hungarian,gerkey2004formal}.
When every UAV observes every task and costs are dominated by travel and capability fit, periodic Hungarian (or auction/CBBA) solvers are strong baselines~\cite{choi2009cbba}.
In that regime, end-to-end reinforcement learning (RL) often \emph{ties} rather than beats classical assignment.

This paper studies a setting where myopic global (or even team-union) matching is the \emph{wrong} inductive bias:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Hard windows:} pop-up attack/recon/intercept tasks expire; misses incur explicit penalties.
  \item \textbf{Delayed / local sensing:} new threats are not globally known; each UAV discovers tasks within a sensing radius or after a delay.
  \item \textbf{Burst arrivals:} correlated pop-ups punish greedy over-commitment of the fleet.
\end{itemize}
We call this suite \textbf{WPS} (Windowed Pop-up Strike).
The fair classical competitor is \textbf{Local-Hungarian}: Hungarian matching on open tasks with edges masked by each agent's known-task set.
Omniscient \textbf{Global-Hungarian} is reported only as an information oracle.

Our methods are \textbf{hybrids}: a learned module reshapes priorities, commit locks, or agent--task pair scores; classical Hungarian (or coalition-aware matching / market baselines) remains the assignment engine on the visibility mask.
This preserves a strong combinatorial core while injecting foresight that pure myopic costs lack.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1)~WPS scenarios and primary metrics \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) / \(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\) focused on perishable and escort outcomes;
(2)~hybrid family with visibility-masked classical assignment: priority+reserve (MLP/Att-RAH), timed commits (Att-Commit), and coalition pair scoring (Att-Coalition);
(3)~100-episode evaluation with paired bootstrap CIs on WPS\_hard (positive Att-RAH vs Local-Hungarian);
(4)~stress and interface suites (WPS\_attn, WPS\_commit, WPS\_escort) reporting where set attention does \emph{not} separate from MLP or market baselines under the same hybrid engine.

\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Classical allocation.}
The Hungarian algorithm~\cite{kuhn1955hungarian} and market-based CBBA~\cite{choi2009cbba} remain standard for multi-robot TA.
Performance-impact (PI) heuristics~\cite{zhao2016pi} score insertions by schedule impact; we use a coalition-aware PI baseline for escort.
Capability-greedy heuristics provide weak baselines.

\textbf{Learning for TA.}
Attention-based policies over task tokens appear in recent multi-agent TA and routing work; our prior TBTA stack uses multi-head attention DQNs over task embeddings for discrete assignment.
Pure RL, however, struggles when the combinatorial core is bipartite matching under dense costs.

\textbf{Hybrids.}
Combining learned scoring with classical solvers (learning-to-rank / cost shaping) is a recurring theme in operations research and robotics.
We adopt that pattern under partial observability and hard deadlines, and extend it to commit locks and pair-level coalition scoring.

\section{Problem Setting: WPS}
We simulate heterogeneous fleets (fighter/recon types) with static Att/Rec missions plus dynamic threats that spawn relative intercept tasks and online Att/Rec arrivals.
WPS configuration knobs include sensing radius \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}\), threat delay \(d\), window length \(W\), burst size, fail rate, and miss/on-time weights.
Local knowledge is tracked per agent; Local-Hungarian forbids assigning an agent to an unknown task.

Locked WPS suites:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{WPS\_easy:} larger radius, longer windows, no burst.
  \item \textbf{WPS\_hard:} tighter windows, stronger delay, bursts (main positive claim).
  \item \textbf{WPS\_burst:} larger bursts and shorter windows.
  \item \textbf{WPS\_attn:} 12 UAVs, scarce F2 specialists, dual-region bursts, no team knowledge sharing.
  \item \textbf{WPS\_commit:} same dual-front asymmetry plus timed per-agent commits and a rematch (reassignment) penalty.
  \item \textbf{WPS\_escort:} protect Rec UAVs via escort tasks; multi-fighter coalition slots; escort score \(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\).
\end{itemize}

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Baselines}
\textbf{Local-Cap-Greedy}, \textbf{Local-CBBA-Replan}, \textbf{Local-Hungarian} use the same visibility constraints.
\textbf{Global-Hungarian} ignores sensing delay (oracle).

\subsection{MLP-RAH}
A compact MLP maps a global feature vector to priorities \(w_t\in[0,1]\) and reserve \(\rho\).
Hungarian costs use
\[
c(a,t)=\mathrm{dist}-\alpha\,\mathrm{cap}-\beta\,w_t-\gamma\,\mathrm{urgency}(t),
\]
with \(\lceil\rho N\rceil\) farthest agents held idle (soft reserve), and unknown edges masked.

\subsection{Att-RAH}
Agent and task tokens (position, type, urgency, scarcity, remaining demand, idle/caps) are encoded with a 2-layer Transformer encoder.
Per-task priorities are read from task tokens; \(\rho\) from a pooled summary.
Allocation remains Local-Hungarian with the same soft reserve and blend of urgency/scarcity/learned score.

\subsection{Att-Commit / MLP-Commit}
On WPS\_commit, the learned module outputs task priorities and per-agent commit locks.
Agents remaining in commit are reserved from rematching; Local-Hungarian assigns only free agents on the visibility mask.
A hand-crafted Urgency-Commit baseline uses the same lock interface without learned priorities.
The rematch penalty enters \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) so free thrashing is costly.

\subsection{Att-Coalition / MLP-Coalition}
On WPS\_escort, open tasks include multi-slot escort missions.
Tokens are built from locally known tasks (urgency/pressure-sorted into a fixed window).
Att-Coalition encodes agents and tasks with self-attention then cross-attention; an MLP head scores each agent--task pair.
MLP-Coalition uses the same tokens and loss without set attention.
Hungarian (coalition-aware residual matching) consumes soft pair scores; selected edges are trained with an actor--critic objective on \(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\) step deltas.
Urgency-Coalition provides a hand-crafted pair-score control.
Classical escort baselines use the same coalition engine: Coalition-Hungarian, Local-CBBA-Coalition~\cite{choi2009cbba}, and Local-PI-Coalition~\cite{zhao2016pi}.

\subsection{Training}
RAH hybrids are trained on WPS\_hard for hundreds of episodes with step reward equal to the delta of \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\), checkpointing on validation \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\).
Commit and Coalition policies are trained on their respective suites with the matching primary score.

\section{Metrics}
Legacy \(F_{\mathrm{Reward}}\) accumulates scaled completion mass plus ad-hoc bonuses and conflates static and perishable success.
We therefore adopt
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{WPS}}
=
12\,n_{\mathrm{on\_time}}
-
30\,n_{\mathrm{missed}}
-
0.01\,\frac{\mathrm{total\_distance}}{\max\_coord}
\label{eq:swps}
\end{equation}
as the \textbf{primary} score for non-escort WPS suites (on-time rate and missed windows as co-primary operational metrics).
On WPS\_commit, rematch cost is included in the reported \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\).
For escort we use
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{ESC}}
=
S_{\mathrm{WPS}}
+
20\,n_{\mathrm{prot\_rec}}
-
30\,n_{\mathrm{recon\_loss}}
+
20\,\mathrm{escort\_coverage},
\label{eq:sesc}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathrm{escort\_coverage}\) is the fraction of escort-required steps with a fighter near the protected recon UAV.
\(F_{\mathrm{Reward}}\) is reported only as a legacy secondary.

\section{Discussion}
The results support a scoped claim: \emph{on WPS\_hard, hybrid cost shaping can significantly improve \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) over myopic Local-Hungarian}.
Reserve is empirically weak.
Set attention is not a free lunch: when the policy only outputs priorities into Hungarian, richer scenes mainly enlarge the information gap to Global-Hungarian without rewarding deeper encoders (WPS\_attn).
Changing the interface to timed commits (WPS\_commit) or agent--task pair scores for coalitions (WPS\_escort) did not reverse that pattern under our training budgets: Att-Commit matched MLP/Urgency, and Att-Coalition underperformed a matched MLP pair scorer while remaining inconclusive versus CBBA.
Future work may need learned communication, joint rolling-horizon plans, or stronger credit assignment if the goal is attention-specific gains rather than hybrid cost shaping alone.

\section{Conclusion}
We reformulated multi-UAV TA evaluation around WPS dynamics and \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) / \(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\).
Att-RAH significantly improves over Local-Hungarian on WPS\_hard.
Multi-front, commit, and escort suites show that set attention does not automatically outperform urgency heuristics, matched MLPs, or market baselines under the same classical assignment engine.
