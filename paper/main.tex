\begin{abstract}
Aerospace multi-UAV mission management often reduces task allocation to bipartite matching when the team shares a complete common operating picture (COP) and costs are dominated by travel and capability fit.
Under time-critical strike and reconnaissance conditions with \emph{delayed cueing}, \emph{local sensing}, \emph{hard mission windows}, burst arrivals, and attrition---here termed Windowed Pop-up Strike (WPS)---visibility-masked myopic matching leaves a large gap to an omniscient COP oracle.
We study hybrids that retain Local-Hungarian while shaping \emph{visibility-masked agent--task edge scores} with matched attention and MLP context encoders (Att-ContextPair / MLP-ContextPair), plus an urgency-pair control.
On the multi-front stress suite WPS\_attn (\(N{=}100\)), Urgency-Pair, MLP-ContextPair, and Att-ContextPair all improve mean \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) over Local-Hungarian (paired bootstrap 95\% CIs exclude zero).
Across three training seeds and two input feature sets, the matched Att-versus-MLP contrast is indistinguishable from zero in every cell, and neither learned encoder separates from the zero-parameter urgency control.
Removing hand-engineered team aggregates from the encoder inputs does not create an attention advantage; it mainly reduces seed variance.
On WPS\_hard, simpler Att-Pair / MLP-Pair shaping does not significantly beat Local-Hungarian.
Results are simulation-only; Global-Hungarian remains an information upper bound.
\end{abstract}

Unmanned aerial vehicles, task allocation, Hungarian algorithm, hybrid learning, partial observability, time windows, mission management

\section{Introduction}
Multi-UAV cooperative missions for intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance (ISR), and time-critical strike require repeated assignment of heterogeneous air vehicles to spatially distributed tasks~\cite{poudel2022survey,shima2006multiple}.
When sensing is global and objectives are myopic travel-versus-capability costs, the Hungarian algorithm~\cite{kuhn1955hungarian} and market-based methods such as the consensus-based bundle algorithm (CBBA)~\cite{choi2009cbba,dias2006market} are strong baselines.
Trajectory-level mixed-integer formulations further couple routing and timing under idealized information~\cite{richards2002aircraft}.
In that regime, replacing the combinatorial core with end-to-end reinforcement learning (RL) has not consistently outperformed classical assignment.

Aerospace command-and-control (C2) settings often violate those assumptions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Hard mission windows:} pop-up attack, reconnaissance, and intercept tasks expire; misses degrade mission value.
  \item \textbf{Incomplete COP:} tasks are disclosed through local sensing radius and cueing delay rather than instantaneous team broadcast.
  \item \textbf{Burst arrivals and attrition:} correlated pop-ups and vehicle loss punish greedy over-commitment.
\end{itemize}
We refer to this family as \textbf{WPS} (Windowed Pop-up Strike).
The fair classical competitor is \textbf{Local-Hungarian}: Hungarian matching restricted to each UAV's known-task set (incomplete COP).
\textbf{Global-Hungarian} ignores sensing limits and is reported only as an omniscient-COP oracle.

\paragraph{Contribution statement.}
We show that visibility-masked edge-cost shaping for Local-Hungarian improves mean \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) over Local-Hungarian on WPS\_attn under paired bootstrap 95\% CIs (Urgency-Pair / MLP-ContextPair / Att-ContextPair), and that under matched capacity, matched optimization, and three training seeds the gain is attributable to edge shaping rather than to attention: neither learned encoder separates from the other, nor from an engineered urgency control.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item An aerospace-oriented WPS scenario family and scores \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\)/\(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\) under incomplete COP, with on-time and miss reported as secondary mission tradeoffs alongside the primary scalar \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\).
  \item Hybrid context$\rightarrow$edge$\rightarrow$Hungarian modules (Att-ContextPair, MLP-ContextPair, Urgency-Pair) trained by mask-aware imitation of Global-Hungarian, with Att-Pair / commit / escort interfaces as related ablations.
  \item Paired-seed evaluation (\(N{=}100\)) over three training seeds and a raw-versus-engineered input ablation: edge shaping beats Local on WPS\_attn, attention-specific superiority over MLP is not supported in any cell, and WPS\_hard remains a hard Local baseline for simpler pair heads.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\textbf{MRTA taxonomies and classical solvers.}
Gerkey and Matari{\'c}~\cite{gerkey2004formal} and Korsah \emph{et al.}~\cite{korsah2013comprehensive} organize multi-robot task allocation (MRTA) by single- versus multi-task robots and instantaneous versus time-extended assignment.
The Hungarian algorithm~\cite{kuhn1955hungarian} remains a standard bipartite engine.
Unlike open-information benchmarks, our Local baselines enforce the same visibility mask as the learned hybrids.

\textbf{Market-based and UAV mission assignment.}
Market methods, including CBBA~\cite{choi2009cbba}, support distributed multi-UAV assignment~\cite{dias2006market,poudel2022survey}.
Extensions address time-sensitive replanning~\cite{chen2021cbba} and schedule-impact scoring (performance impact, PI)~\cite{zhao2016pi}.
Genetic and optimization-based UAV assignment formulations target cooperating uninhabited vehicles under coupling constraints~\cite{shima2006multiple}.
Recent aerospace work studies communication-aware and learning-augmented swarm allocation~\cite{xiong2026cata}.
We retain CBBA/PI as Local baselines but evaluate them under the same incomplete-COP mask as Hungarian hybrids.

\textbf{Planning under timing and information constraints.}
Mixed-integer trajectory planning illustrates the cost of enforcing collision and timing constraints when models are centralized~\cite{richards2002aircraft}.
Our focus is discrete assignment under delayed disclosure rather than continuous trajectory optimization.

\textbf{Learning and hybrids.}
End-to-end policies that select discrete assignments, including attention over tokens~\cite{vaswani2017attention}, must jointly solve scoring and combinatorial feasibility.
When matching structure is available, hybrids that learn scores for a classical solver are a recurring pattern.
We adopt that pattern under partial observability: networks output priorities (or locks/pair scores), while conflict resolution remains classical.
Prior TBTA experiments in a PettingZoo environment~\cite{terry2021pettingzoo} motivate keeping Hungarian as the assignment engine.

\section{System Model and WPS Scenarios}
\subsection{Agents, tasks, and information}
Let \(\mathcal{A}_t\) be the set of live UAVs and \(\mathcal{T}_t\) the set of open tasks at discrete time \(t\).
Each agent \(a\in\mathcal{A}_t\) maintains a known-task set \(\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\subseteq\mathcal{T}_t\) updated by sensing radius \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}\) and threat-disclosure delay \(d\).
A \emph{local} allocator may assign \(a\) only to tasks in \(\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\) (incomplete COP).
Heterogeneous fighter/recon types constrain capability; intercept and escort tasks may require multiple agents.

\subsection{Mission dynamics}
Episodes combine static strike/recon missions with dynamic threats that spawn intercepts and online arrivals.
Configurable parameters include \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}\), \(d\), window length \(W\), burst size, fail rate, and evaluation weights.
Table~\ref{tab:suites} summarizes locked suites.
WPS\_hard is the primary confirmatory suite; WPS\_attn stresses multi-front asymmetry; WPS\_commit and WPS\_escort are interface extensions (Appendix~\ref{app:ext}).
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{WPS\_commit:} dual-front arrivals, timed \texttt{commit\_horizon}, and rematch penalty folded into \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\).
  \item \textbf{WPS\_escort:} moving escort tasks, multi-fighter residual slots, and escort score \(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\) (Section~V).
\end{itemize}

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Baselines}
Local-Cap-Greedy, Local-CBBA-Replan~\cite{choi2009cbba,chen2021cbba}, and Local-Hungarian share the visibility mask and comparable replan triggers.
Global-Hungarian omits the mask (oracle).
Escort experiments additionally use Local-PI-Coalition~\cite{zhao2016pi} and Local-CBBA-Coalition under multi-slot residual matching (Appendix~\ref{app:ext}).

\subsection{MLP-RAH and Att-RAH (task-priority interface)}
As a legacy interface, MLP-RAH / Att-RAH map state to per-task priorities \(w\in[0,1]^{|\mathcal{T}_t|}\) and reserve \(\rho\), reshaping
\begin{equation}
c(a,t)=\mathrm{dist}(a,t)-\alpha\,\mathrm{cap}(a,t)-\beta\,w_t-\gamma\,\mathrm{urgency}(t)
\label{eq:cost}
\end{equation}
on known pairs only.
Att-RAH uses a 2-layer Transformer encoder over agent/task tokens; MLP-RAH uses a flat MLP.
We retain this interface as an ablation; the primary WPS\_hard campaign below uses bipartite edge scores.

\subsection{Pair-cost hybrids (primary interface)}
Att-ContextPair and MLP-ContextPair output scores \(s(a,t)\) on visibility-valid edges and inject
\(c'(a,t)=c_{\mathrm{base}}(a,t)-s(a,t)\) into Local-Hungarian (\(|s|\le 0.35\)).
Both consume the same agent/task tokens plus an 8-D situation summary (urgency count, dual-front imbalance, free/fighter fractions, time).
\textbf{Att-ContextPair} uses self- and cross-attention with a context-biased pair head~\cite{vaswani2017attention}.
\textbf{MLP-ContextPair} is a capacity-matched MLP on concatenated edge features and the same summary.
\textbf{Urgency-Pair} uses engineered urgency/scarcity/distance residuals (no learning).
To test whether attention \emph{discovers} relational context rather than reading it off engineered inputs, we also train a \textbf{raw} variant of both encoders in which every team aggregate is deleted from the inputs: task scarcity, known-by count, nearest-specialist distance and region identifier are removed from the task tokens, the known-urgent count is removed from the agent tokens, and the situation summary is reduced to the mission clock.
Both encoders then see only per-entity attributes and must infer contention themselves.
Training: Phase~A imitates Global-Hungarian on \emph{visible} edges (900 episodes on WPS\_attn); Phase~B RL fine-tune is admitted only if Phase~A beats Local on \(N{=}100\) (gate); deployed checkpoints are best-of IL/RL by confirmatory eval.
Simpler Att-Pair / MLP-Pair heads without the explicit context summary are reported on WPS\_hard as a related campaign.

\subsection{Timed commits (Att-Commit)}
Att-Commit, MLP-Commit, and Urgency-Commit output task priorities and per-agent commit locks.
Locked agents keep their assignment until the commit horizon expires; Local-Hungarian runs only on free agents under the same visibility mask.
Training and evaluation use WPS\_commit (Appendix~\ref{app:ext}).

\subsection{Coalition escort (Att-Coalition)}
For WPS\_escort, Att-Coalition / MLP-Coalition / Urgency-Coalition score agent--task pairs for coalition (multi-slot residual) Hungarian matching, analogous to Att-Pair but with escort features.
Classical escort baselines include Coalition-Hungarian, Local-CBBA-Coalition, and Local-PI-Coalition~\cite{zhao2016pi}.

\subsection{Training}
ContextPair on WPS\_attn: 900 imitation episodes then gated RL (350 episodes) with Adam; validation every 40 episodes on Local-policy \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\).
Imitation uses mini-batches of 8 replan states with linear learning-rate warmup over the first 50 updates, identical for both encoders; an earlier single-state update rule is reported in Sec.~\ref{sec:optconfound} because it materially changed the Att-versus-MLP conclusion.
Each encoder--feature-set cell is trained with three seeds (0--2) and evaluated independently.
WPS\_hard Att-Pair / MLP-Pair: shorter IL$\rightarrow$RL as previously reported.
Inference uses periodic replanning (interval 20) plus event triggers.
Further details appear in Appendix~\ref{app:repro}.

\section{Evaluation Metrics}
We treat \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) as an \emph{evaluation score} encoding preference for on-time completions over misses, with a small travel regularizer aligned to WPS\_hard weights:
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{WPS}}
=
12\,n_{\mathrm{on\_time}}
-
30\,n_{\mathrm{missed}}
-
0.01\,\frac{\mathrm{total\_distance}}{\max\_coord}.
\label{eq:swps}
\end{equation}
On-time rate and missed-window count are co-primary operational metrics.
For escort missions,
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{ESC}}
=
S_{\mathrm{WPS}}
+
20\,n_{\mathrm{prot\_rec}}
-
30\,n_{\mathrm{recon\_loss}}
+
20\,\mathrm{escort\_coverage},
\label{eq:sesc}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathrm{escort\_coverage}\) is the fraction of escort-required steps with a fighter near the protected recon UAV.
Weights are heuristic; sensitivity under alternate weightings is reported in Appendix~\ref{app:sens}.
A legacy completion-mass score is recorded only as a secondary diagnostic.

\subsection{Attention versus MLP under matched controls}
\label{sec:optconfound}
Table~\ref{tab:seeds} resolves the architecture question over three training seeds and both input feature sets.
The paired Att$-$MLP contrast has a 95\% CI containing zero in all six cells, and the pooled contrasts (\(-6.4\) raw, \(+6.7\) engineered) are likewise indistinguishable from zero.
The point estimate changes sign between feature sets and between seeds within the engineered set, which is the expected behavior of an effect that is small relative to training noise rather than an architectural property.

Two controls make this reading concrete.
First, an earlier version of this study updated the imitation loss on one replan state at a time; under that rule the same comparison produced \(\widehat{\Delta}_{\mathrm{Att-MLP}}\approx-20\) with a CI excluding zero, i.e.\ an apparently significant MLP advantage.
Replacing single-state updates with mini-batches and warmup---a change to the optimizer alone, with both architectures, both feature sets, and all evaluation seeds held fixed---removed that effect.
We therefore retract it: it measured an optimization confound that penalized the Transformer, not a property of set attention.
Second, deleting the engineered team aggregates leaves attention statistically unchanged (\(-3.6\), CI \([-27.4,+18.8]\) on seed 0), consistent with the encoder recovering equivalent information from the raw tokens; the visible effect of the deletion is on stability, with across-seed standard deviation falling from \(8.9\)/\(11.7\) to \(2.7\)/\(2.1\) for Att/MLP.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
On WPS\_attn, visibility-masked edge-cost shaping improves \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) and secondary on-time/miss metrics over Local-Hungarian under paired bootstrap CIs.
The gain is shared by the engineered Urgency-Pair control and by both learned encoders, and no pairwise contrast among the three excludes zero, so the result supports \emph{hybrid cost shaping} rather than set attention or learning as such.
On WPS\_hard, myopic Local-Hungarian remains competitive against simpler pair heads.
The large Global oracle gap indicates incomplete COP still dominates encoder choice.

The retracted Att$-$MLP effect of Sec.~\ref{sec:optconfound} carries a methodological lesson beyond this study.
Architecture comparisons in learned allocation are frequently reported at a single training seed with a shared optimizer configuration, and Transformers and MLPs do not respond identically to batch size or warmup.
Here an optimizer detail produced a significant architecture claim in the wrong direction, of a magnitude comparable to the genuine effect of edge shaping itself.
Matched optimization and multiple seeds should be treated as prerequisites for such claims, not refinements.

\textbf{Limitations.}
Results are limited to discrete-time simulation without flight or hardware-in-the-loop validation.
\(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) is a scalarization of mission tradeoffs (on-time, miss, distance); Appendix~\ref{app:sens} reports weight sensitivity.
Training budgets are fixed; RL did not improve confirmatory Att/MLP scores over imitation.
Three training seeds bound but do not eliminate seed sensitivity, and checkpoints are selected by best validation score, which biases individual cells upward; the Att$-$MLP contrasts are affected symmetrically.
Attention is evaluated at the scale of this suite (12 agents, tens of open tasks); asymptotic advantages at larger team sizes are untested.
Secondary suites are not multiplicity-adjusted.
Communication impairments beyond knowledge masking are not modeled.

\section{Conclusion}
Under WPS\_attn, context-aware edge shaping for Local-Hungarian significantly improves \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) over visibility-masked Local-Hungarian, but the improvement is not attributable to attention: across three seeds and two input feature sets, a matched attention encoder is indistinguishable from an MLP control and from an engineered urgency rule.
Under WPS\_hard, simpler Att-Pair / MLP-Pair shaping does not significantly beat Local-Hungarian.
For aerospace mission management, retain classical assignment under incomplete COP and require paired gains---under matched optimization, matched non-attention controls, and multiple training seeds---before claiming attention advantages.
