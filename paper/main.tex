\begin{abstract}
Aerospace multi-UAV mission management often reduces task allocation to bipartite matching when the team shares a complete common operating picture (COP).
Under time-critical strike and reconnaissance with \emph{local sensing}, \emph{delayed cueing}, \emph{hard mission windows}, burst arrivals, and attrition---Windowed Pop-up Strike (WPS)---visibility-masked Local-Hungarian leaves a large gap to an omniscient Global-Hungarian oracle.
We show two co-equal results.
First, that gap is primarily \emph{informational}: under idealized full COP (AWACS), Local-Hungarian equals Global; increasing sensing radius or shortening team cueing delay closes the gap monotonically, whereas oversizing the fleet under incomplete COP raises idle fraction with little Local gain.
Second, under the incomplete-COP mask, hybrid \emph{edge-cost shaping} for Local-Hungarian (Urgency-Pair and matched Att / MLP / GNN ContextPair) improves mean \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) on WPS\_attn (\(N{=}100\); paired bootstrap 95\% CIs exclude zero), while Local-CBBA and Local-PI do not; Att, MLP, GNN, and Urgency remain mutually indistinguishable under matched optimization and three training seeds.
Architecture and scale transfers do not separate attention from MLP.
Results are simulation-only; code and experiment scripts will be released upon publication (URL withheld for double-blind review).
% CAMERA-READY: code is available at \url{https://github.com/andrekuros/Multi-UAV-TA-gym-env}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Multi-UAV cooperative missions for intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance (ISR), and time-critical strike require repeated assignment of heterogeneous air vehicles to spatially distributed tasks~\cite{poudel2022survey,shima2006multiple}.
When sensing is global and objectives are myopic travel-versus-capability costs, the Hungarian algorithm~\cite{kuhn1955hungarian} and market-based methods such as the consensus-based bundle algorithm (CBBA)~\cite{choi2009cbba,dias2006market} are strong baselines.
Trajectory-level mixed-integer formulations further couple routing and timing under idealized information~\cite{richards2002aircraft}.
In that regime, replacing the combinatorial core with end-to-end reinforcement learning (RL) has not consistently outperformed classical assignment.

Aerospace command-and-control (C2) settings often violate those assumptions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Hard mission windows:} pop-up attack, reconnaissance, and intercept tasks expire; misses degrade mission value.
  \item \textbf{Incomplete COP:} tasks are disclosed through local sensing radius and cueing delay rather than instantaneous team broadcast.
  \item \textbf{Burst arrivals and attrition:} correlated pop-ups and vehicle loss punish greedy over-commitment.
\end{itemize}
We refer to this family as \textbf{WPS} (Windowed Pop-up Strike).
The fair classical competitor is \textbf{Local-Hungarian}: Hungarian matching restricted to each UAV's known-task set (incomplete COP).
\textbf{Global-Hungarian} ignores sensing limits and is reported only as an omniscient-COP oracle.
The open simulation stack that implements these masks is summarized in Sec.~\ref{sec:env}.

\paragraph{Contribution statement.}
We characterize the Local--Global \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) gap as a COP / information problem (AWACS equality, sensing and cueing sweeps, oversized-fleet idle), and show that under incomplete COP the practical mitigation is visibility-masked edge-cost shaping for Local-Hungarian---not a particular encoder architecture or classical market replanner under the same mask.
An open WPS simulation stack enables fair masked baselines and reproducible evaluation (enabling artifact; release upon publication).

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Enabling artifact:} an open PettingZoo multi-UAV environment with a locked WPS scenario registry, explicit visibility masks (\texttt{agent\_known\_ids}), classical/hybrid allocator plug-ins, eval scripts, and an optional 3D replay dashboard (URL withheld for double-blind review; released upon publication).
  % CAMERA-READY: (\url{https://github.com/andrekuros/Multi-UAV-TA-gym-env}).
  \item COP diagnostics on that stack: full-COP AWACS, sensing-radius and team-cueing sweeps, and fixed-load fleet oversizing with idle reporting, alongside scores \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\)/\(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\).
  \item Hybrid context$\rightarrow$edge$\rightarrow$Hungarian modules (Att- / MLP- / GNN-ContextPair, Urgency-Pair) trained by mask-aware imitation of Global-Hungarian, evaluated against Local-Hungarian, Local-CBBA-Replan, and Local-PI under a shared visibility mask.
  \item Matched Att / MLP / GNN / Urgency negatives (including an optimizer-confound retraction and scale transfer), establishing hybrid shaping rather than architecture as the mitigation claim on WPS\_attn; WPS\_hard remains a boundary where simpler pair heads do not beat Local.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\textbf{MRTA taxonomies and classical solvers.}
Gerkey and Matari{\'c}~\cite{gerkey2004formal} and Korsah \emph{et al.}~\cite{korsah2013comprehensive} organize multi-robot task allocation (MRTA) by single- versus multi-task robots and instantaneous versus time-extended assignment.
The Hungarian algorithm~\cite{kuhn1955hungarian} remains a standard bipartite engine.
Unlike open-information benchmarks, our Local baselines enforce the same visibility mask as the learned hybrids.

\textbf{Market-based and UAV mission assignment.}
Market methods, including CBBA~\cite{choi2009cbba}, support distributed multi-UAV assignment~\cite{dias2006market,poudel2022survey}.
Extensions address time-sensitive replanning~\cite{chen2021cbba} and schedule-impact scoring (performance impact, PI)~\cite{zhao2016pi}.
Genetic and optimization-based UAV assignment formulations target cooperating uninhabited vehicles under coupling constraints~\cite{shima2006multiple}.
Recent aerospace work studies communication-aware and learning-augmented swarm allocation~\cite{xiong2026cata}.
We retain CBBA/PI as Local baselines but evaluate them under the same incomplete-COP mask as Hungarian hybrids.

\textbf{Planning under timing and information constraints.}
Mixed-integer trajectory planning illustrates the cost of enforcing collision and timing constraints when models are centralized~\cite{richards2002aircraft}.
Our focus is discrete assignment under delayed disclosure rather than continuous trajectory optimization.

\textbf{Communication and incomplete information in UAV assignment.}
Market and learning allocators are sensitive to non-ideal links (delay, loss, bit errors) and communication-aware policies~\cite{cao2025communication,raja2021communication,xiong2026cata}.
Time-sensitive CBBA variants address replanning under operational tempo~\cite{chen2021cbba}.
Unlike studies that emphasize message protocols or transmission gating under shared task catalogs, we fix a visibility mask \(\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\) (sensing radius / cueing delay / share flag) and compare classical and hybrid solvers under that \emph{same} incomplete COP.

\textbf{Learning and hybrids.}
End-to-end policies that select discrete assignments, including attention over tokens~\cite{vaswani2017attention}, must jointly solve scoring and combinatorial feasibility.
When matching structure is available, hybrids that learn scores for a classical solver are a recurring pattern.
We adopt that pattern under partial observability: networks output pair scores, while conflict resolution remains classical.
Prior TBTA experiments in a PettingZoo environment~\cite{terry2021pettingzoo} motivate keeping Hungarian as the assignment engine.

\textbf{Open simulators and assignment benchmarks.}
PettingZoo and related multi-agent APIs provide general MARL interfaces~\cite{terry2021pettingzoo}, but rarely encode aerospace incomplete-COP disclosure, perishable windows, and a shared classical/hybrid allocator contract.
Our release couples a WPS scenario registry to a visibility-masked assignment API so Local-Hungarian, market baselines, and cost-shaping hybrids are compared under the same \(\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\) mask, with optional 3D replay for qualitative mission inspection.

\section{System Model and WPS Scenarios}
\subsection{Agents, tasks, and information}
Let \(\mathcal{A}_t\) be the set of live UAVs and \(\mathcal{T}_t\) the set of open tasks at discrete time \(t\).
Each agent \(a\in\mathcal{A}_t\) maintains a known-task set \(\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\subseteq\mathcal{T}_t\) updated by sensing radius \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}\) and threat-disclosure delay \(d\).
A \emph{local} allocator may assign \(a\) only to tasks in \(\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\) (incomplete COP).
Heterogeneous fighter/recon types constrain capability; intercept and escort tasks may require multiple agents.

\paragraph{Local assignment problem.}
At each replan, Local-Hungarian seeks a conflict-free matching on the visibility-valid bipartite graph
with edge costs \(c(a,t)\) built from travel distance and capability fit (and optional shaping terms in Sec.~\ref{sec:methods}):
\begin{equation}
\min_{\pi}\;\sum_{(a,t)\in\pi} c(a,t)
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
t\in\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\ \forall (a,t)\in\pi,
\label{eq:localassign}
\end{equation}
with capacity constraints induced by live agents and open tasks.
Global-Hungarian drops the \(t\in\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\) restriction (oracle COP).
Episode quality is scored by \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) (Sec.~\ref{sec:metrics}), which privileges on-time completions over misses and is \emph{not} identical to the myopic Hungarian objective in~\eqref{eq:localassign}.

\subsection{Mission dynamics}
Episodes combine static strike/recon missions with dynamic threats that spawn intercepts and online arrivals.
Configurable parameters include \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}\), \(d\), window length \(W\), burst size, fail rate, and evaluation weights.
Table~\ref{tab:suites} summarizes locked suites.
WPS\_attn is the primary multi-front stress suite (incomplete COP); WPS\_attn\_AWACS is the full-COP diagnostic; WPS\_hard is a related boundary suite; WPS\_commit and WPS\_escort are interface extensions (Appendix~\ref{app:ext}).
Under local-only sensing (\texttt{share\_knowledge=False}), discovery is radius-limited and cueing delay does not broadcast; team cueing uses \texttt{share\_knowledge=True} with \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}{=}0\).

\subsection{Simulation environment and software framework}
\label{sec:env}
Quantitative results use an open multi-UAV stack released with the manuscript
(URL withheld for double-blind review; released upon publication)~\cite{multi_uav_ta_gym}.
% CAMERA-READY: at \url{https://github.com/andrekuros/Multi-UAV-TA-gym-env}~\cite{multi_uav_ta_gym}.
Episodes run in a PettingZoo \texttt{ParallelEnv} (\texttt{MultiUAVEnv}) configured by \texttt{agentEnvOptions} and a locked scenario registry (Table~\ref{tab:suites}).
Geometry and line-of-sight helpers are accelerated in a Rust \texttt{core\_sim} module; training and batch evaluation remain headless.

\paragraph{Simulation model.}
The world is a planar mission area of fixed width/height; agent and task positions are 2-D (with a constant display altitude in the replay UI).
Each UAV type (\(F1/F2\) fighters, \(R1/R2\) recon) has a type-dependent maximum speed, engagement range, and capability vector over task classes (attack, recon, intercept, hold/escort).
Discrete time steps advance navigation toward the assigned task at the agent's max speed; upon arrival the agent executes a type-dependent task duration before becoming free for rematch.
Tasks include static strike/recon missions and dynamic intercepts spawned from threat groups: with configured arrival and fail rates, burst arrivals, and optional dual-front regions.
Hard windows attach a deadline of length \(W\) at task creation; on-time completion and misses update \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\).
Attrition removes agents according to type-dependent fail multipliers when a fail event fires.

\paragraph{Incomplete COP and allocator API.}
Incomplete COP is first-class: each agent maintains \(\mathcal{K}_{a,t}\) via sensing radius \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}\), cueing delay \(d\), and \texttt{share\_knowledge}.
Under local-only sensing, pop-ups are discovered when an agent comes within \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}\); under team cueing, tasks broadcast after delay \(d\).
The env exposes \texttt{agent\_visibility\_map()}, which Local allocators consume as \texttt{agent\_known\_ids} so unknown agent--task edges are masked.
Global-Hungarian omits the mask (oracle).
Hybrids only reshape edge scores or priorities, then call the same Hungarian engine under the mask.
Market baselines (Local-CBBA-Replan, Local-PI) use the identical visibility interface and comparable replan triggers.
Episode outcomes feed evaluation scores \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) / \(S_{\mathrm{ESC}}\) (Sec.~\ref{sec:metrics}).

Figure~\ref{fig:envui} shows the optional React / Three.js replay dashboard used for qualitative inspection of assignments, threats, and windows; it is not part of the training loop.

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}
\subsection{Baselines}
Local-Cap-Greedy, Local-CBBA-Replan~\cite{choi2009cbba,chen2021cbba}, Local-PI~\cite{zhao2016pi}, and Local-Hungarian share the visibility mask (\texttt{agent\_known\_ids}) and comparable replan triggers.
Global-Hungarian omits the mask (oracle).

\subsection{Pair-cost hybrids (primary interface)}
The primary learned interface outputs scores \(s(a,t)\) on visibility-valid edges and injects
\(c'(a,t)=c_{\mathrm{base}}(a,t)-s(a,t)\) into Local-Hungarian (\(|s|\le 0.35\)).
Att-ContextPair, MLP-ContextPair, and GNN-ContextPair consume the same agent/task tokens plus a situation summary (engineered 8-D, or mission clock only in the raw ablation).
\textbf{Att-ContextPair} uses self- and cross-attention with a context-biased pair head~\cite{vaswani2017attention}.
\textbf{MLP-ContextPair} is a capacity-matched MLP on concatenated edge features and the same summary.
\textbf{GNN-ContextPair} runs \(L{=}2\) bipartite message-passing layers on the visibility-valid agent--task adjacency (pure PyTorch; no external GNN library), then the same context-biased pair head.
\textbf{Urgency-Pair} uses engineered urgency/scarcity/distance residuals (no learning).
To test whether relational encoders \emph{discover} context rather than reading it off engineered inputs, we also train a \textbf{raw} variant in which every team aggregate is deleted from the inputs: task scarcity, known-by count, nearest-specialist distance and region identifier are removed from the task tokens, the known-urgent count is removed from the agent tokens, and the situation summary is reduced to the mission clock.

\paragraph{Training and IL--metric gap.}
Phase~A imitates Global-Hungarian on \emph{visible} edges (900 episodes on WPS\_attn) with mini-batches of 8 replan states and linear learning-rate warmup over the first 50 updates (identical for Att / MLP / GNN).
Phase~B RL fine-tune is admitted only if Phase~A beats Local on \(N{=}100\); deployed checkpoints are best-of IL/RL by confirmatory eval.
An earlier single-state update rule is reported in Sec.~\ref{sec:optconfound} because it materially changed the Att-versus-MLP conclusion.
Each encoder--feature-set cell is trained with three seeds (0--2).
Inference uses periodic replanning (interval 20) plus event triggers.
Importantly, imitation targets myopic Global matching on visible edges, whereas reported quality is \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) (Sec.~\ref{sec:metrics}).
The shaping claim does not require perfect IL--metric alignment: zero-parameter Urgency-Pair still improves \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) over Local under the same mask (Table~\ref{tab:attn}).
Simpler Att-Pair / MLP-Pair heads without the explicit context summary are reported on WPS\_hard as a related campaign.

\subsection{Legacy and extension interfaces}
Task-priority RAH (MLP/Att), timed commits, and coalition escort change the decision interface while retaining classical conflict resolution under the same visibility mask.
We treat them as ablations and extensions; algorithms, training, and tables appear in Appendix~\ref{app:ext}.

\section{Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}
We treat \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) as an \emph{evaluation score} encoding preference for on-time completions over misses, with a small travel regularizer aligned to WPS\_hard weights:
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{WPS}}
=
12\,n_{\mathrm{on\_time}}
-
30\,n_{\mathrm{missed}}
-
0.01\,\frac{\mathrm{total\_distance}}{\max\_coord}.
\label{eq:swps}
\end{equation}
On-time rate and missed-window count are co-primary operational metrics.
For escort missions,
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{ESC}}
=
S_{\mathrm{WPS}}
+
20\,n_{\mathrm{prot\_rec}}
-
30\,n_{\mathrm{recon\_loss}}
+
20\,\mathrm{escort\_coverage},
\label{eq:sesc}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathrm{escort\_coverage}\) is the fraction of escort-required steps with a fighter near the protected recon UAV.
Weights are heuristic; sensitivity under alternate weightings is reported in Appendix~\ref{app:sens}.
A legacy completion-mass score is recorded only as a secondary diagnostic.

\section{Experiments}
\subsection{Protocol}
Unless noted, each method--suite pair uses \(N{=}100\) episodes with seeds \(0,\ldots,99\).
For comparisons to Local-Hungarian we report the paired mean difference and a percentile bootstrap 95\% CI (2000--4000 resamples).
COP diagnostics use Local / Global / Urgency-Pair; the shaping campaign pools Att / MLP / GNN over three training seeds (raw ContextPair IL).

\subsection{Architecture and optimization controls}
\label{sec:optconfound}
Table~\ref{tab:seeds} resolves Att versus MLP over three training seeds and both input feature sets.
The paired Att$-$MLP contrast has a 95\% CI containing zero in all six cells; pooled contrasts (\(-6.4\) raw, \(+6.7\) engineered) are likewise indistinguishable from zero.

Two controls make this reading concrete.
First, an earlier single-state imitation update produced \(\widehat{\Delta}_{\mathrm{Att-MLP}}\approx-20\) with a CI excluding zero; mini-batches and warmup---optimizer alone---removed that effect, so we retract it as an optimization confound.
Second, deleting engineered team aggregates leaves attention statistically unchanged on seed 0, consistent with recovering equivalent information from raw tokens.

A scale-transfer control (Table~\ref{tab:scale}) likewise fails to separate Att from MLP or from Urgency-Pair, though both encoders still beat Local at L/XL.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
Two mission-management levers dominate this study (Sec.~\ref{sec:cop}).
\textbf{COP quality} sets the Local--Global ceiling: AWACS equality and the sensing/cueing sweeps show that incomplete information---not Hungarian solver quality---explains most of the oracle gap, and oversizing fleets without better sensing/sharing yields idle airframes.
\textbf{Hybrid edge shaping} under the mask recovers a smaller but reliable fraction of that gap on WPS\_attn for Urgency-Pair and ContextPair alike; Local-CBBA / Local-PI do not.
Encoder architecture (Att / MLP / GNN) is not the lever once optimization and seeds are matched.
The IL objective (Global matching on visible edges) is not identical to \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\); we therefore emphasize Urgency-Pair and paired CIs rather than treating imitation loss as a proxy for mission score.

The retracted Att$-$MLP effect of Sec.~\ref{sec:optconfound} is a methodological lesson: architecture comparisons at a single seed with a shared optimizer can manufacture false claims of magnitude comparable to genuine shaping gains.

\textbf{Limitations.}
Simulation only; AWACS is idealized full COP; sensing and cueing sweeps are 1-D; idle uses env \texttt{reserve\_idle\_fraction}.
\(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) scalarization sensitivity is in Appendix~\ref{app:sens}.
RL Phase~B did not improve confirmatory Att/MLP over imitation.
Three seeds bound but do not eliminate seed sensitivity.
Communication impairments beyond knowledge masking are not modeled.

\section{Conclusion}
Under WPS, the Local--Global assignment gap is primarily a COP problem: full information makes Local-Hungarian equal Global-Hungarian, and improving sensing radius or cueing delay closes the gap, whereas extra UAVs under incomplete COP mostly idle.
Under incomplete COP, visibility-masked edge-cost shaping for Local-Hungarian significantly improves \(S_{\mathrm{WPS}}\) on WPS\_attn, but the gain is shared by Urgency-Pair and matched Att / MLP / GNN ContextPair and is not recovered by Local-CBBA or Local-PI.
For aerospace C2, invest first in sensing and sharing, then in cost shaping under the mask, and require matched non-attention controls before claiming architectural advantages.

\subsection{Task-priority RAH (legacy)}
MLP-RAH / Att-RAH map state to per-task priorities \(w\in[0,1]^{|\mathcal{T}_t|}\) and reserve \(\rho\), reshaping
\begin{equation}
c(a,t)=\mathrm{dist}(a,t)-\alpha\,\mathrm{cap}(a,t)-\beta\,w_t-\gamma\,\mathrm{urgency}(t)
\label{eq:cost}
\end{equation}
on known pairs only.
Att-RAH uses a 2-layer Transformer encoder; MLP-RAH uses a flat MLP.
We retain this interface only as a historical ablation; the primary campaign uses ContextPair edge scores (Sec.~\ref{sec:methods}).

\subsection{Timed commits and coalition escort}
Timed commits (WPS\_commit) and coalition escort (WPS\_escort) change the decision interface while keeping classical conflict resolution under the same visibility mask.
